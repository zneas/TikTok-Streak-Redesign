# TikTok Streak Prototype — Tester Guide

> Note for developers: this guide was written for the Figma prototype (the "pick the flow" and "press R" steps are Figma-only). The five tasks are what the coded MVP must support.

Try our TikTok Streak redesign, then submit your team's evaluation. It takes about 20 minutes. We're testing the design, not you.

## Get started

1. Open the prototype link from the presenting team and pick the flow **Redesign · 1 Home → Inbox → Maya**.
2. Click to tap, scroll to see more, and tap the dimmed area to close a sheet.
3. To press and hold, hold the click and move the mouse slightly.
4. Press R to restart before each task. Only the parts needed for the tasks work, and all people and videos are made up.

## Tasks

Use these as a guide, starting each one from Home:

1. Find which of Maya's videos you've already responded to, then watch the newest one.
2. Find out what counts as a streak day with Maya.
3. Your streak count with Maya stresses you out. Hide it for you only, without telling Maya.
4. Check on your shared pet and find out what helps it grow.
5. You have an exam Saturday. Set up your streak with Maya so it puts less pressure on you.

## Evaluation

In a Google Sheet, one tab per team, write **Prototype:** TikTok Streak Redesign and **Group evaluator:** your team name. Then answer:

- **I want...** what you wanted to do or find
- **I need...** what you needed to finish a task
- **I wish...** what would make it better
- **I don't like...** what frustrated or confused you
- **Overall rating:** 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy)
